        The first time you open it

        Your system will warn you. That’s normal.

        Lexio is open source and doesn't pay for Apple or Microsoft certificates. So your system asks you to confirm once.

        macOS

        1. Open the .dmg and drag Lexio to Applications.
        2. Open it. A warning shows up: click Done.
        3. System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
        If you’re technical 
        xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Lexio.app

        Windows

        1. Open the installer.
        2. “Windows protected your PC” shows up: click More info.
        3. Click Run anyway.

        After that it updates itself.
